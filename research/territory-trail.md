**Best match: Paper.io 2.** Leave your home area, drag a trail, loop back to claim the enclosed space, cut rivals' trails to defeat them. Splix.io uses the same rules on a square grid.

## 2. Core loop (CORE)

You start with a small patch of your color. Leaving it drags a trail behind you. Returning to your own color turns everything the trail enclosed into your land, empty space and rival land alike. While outside you are exposed, and anyone who touches your trail defeats you. Grow, steal, cut rivals, and avoid being cut until you are defeated or the map is cleared.

paint burst around you; cone blast ahead; paint-ball projectile that kills on impact; mines; speckled trail; spiral shockwave that kills nearby enemies and clears their paint; painting dash; speed on own land; speed off own land; speed everywhere; shock field; shield wall rivals cannot cross; solid tail that blocks rivals; wider view; item radar on minimap; "Start x5 bigger" starting land (rewarded ad).
