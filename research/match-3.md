# Match-3 genre feature list (Camalot research)

Researched 2026-11-16. Quick pass, not exhaustive. Tags: CORE / COMMON / EXTRA (named game). "Unverified" = not confirmed from a fetched source.

## 1. Top games

| Game | Maker | Platform | Where kids find it | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fishdom | Playrix | iOS, Android, Windows, Mac (match-3 mobile version since Dec 2015) | App Store / Google Play | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fishdom ; https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fishdom/id664575829 |
| Homescapes (and sibling Gardenscapes) | Playrix | iOS, Android, Windows, macOS | App stores | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homescapes |
| Candy Crush Saga | King | iOS, Android, web/Facebook | App stores | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candy_Crush_Saga |

**Fishdom confirmed.** Match-3 levels pay out coins. The player spends coins on fish, decorations and "comfort" items to build themed aquariums. In the original PC version, three progress meters awarded bronze, silver and gold trophies. The mobile version adds pets as rewards, events (expeditions, season adventures with keys and card sets), and premium currency (diamonds). App Store: free, in-app purchases $0.99-$15.99, a purchasable "fishy bank" (piggy bank), age 13+, loot boxes and chat listed. User reviews report no ads. Fish feeding and care: unverified.

Note: CrazyGames' "match-3" tag mixes in merge, mahjong and tile-triple games. Those are a different genre.

## 2. Core loop
Pick the next level on a map. Swap neighbouring pieces to make matches until the level goals are met before moves run out. A win pays stars and/or coins. Stars unlock the next renovation task in the story (fix a room, add an aquarium decoration). Coins buy boosters and lives. A loss costs a life. **CORE** (match loop); meta loop **COMMON** (Fishdom, Homescapes, Gardenscapes, Royal Match; not Candy Crush).

## 3. Controls
- Swipe or drag a piece onto a neighbour (touch), or click-drag / click-click (mouse). **CORE**
- Single tap fires a special piece in place (Homescapes v5.3.2+). **COMMON**
- Tap a booster icon, then tap the target tile. **COMMON**

## 4. Rules
- Swap only orthogonal neighbours. The swap counts only if it makes a match of 3 or more; otherwise it reverts. **CORE**
- Matched pieces clear, pieces above fall, new pieces drop in from the top. **CORE**
- Cascades: chain matches from falling pieces score progressively more. **CORE**
- Move limit per level (Homescapes cut most levels to 25 moves or fewer in 2021). **COMMON** (the standard)
- Timer instead of moves: **EXTRA / unverified** (seen in some web clones; not confirmed)
- Auto-shuffle when no valid move exists: **COMMON**, unverified
- Leftover moves become bonus specials at level end ("sugar crush"-style): **COMMON**, unverified
- Level goal types:
  - reach a score / star thresholds (stars awarded by score in Candy Crush) **COMMON**
  - clear coloured tile layers (jelly, carpet; Homescapes's carpet *spreads*) **COMMON**
  - collect N pieces of a colour **COMMON**
  - drop items to the bottom row (Candy Crush ingredients) **COMMON**
  - clear N blockers (vases, boxes) **COMMON**
  - "make special combos" orders **EXTRA** (Candy Crush, rare)

## 5. Special pieces (generic names)
| Match shape | Creates | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 4 in a line | Line clearer | Clears its row or column. Direction comes from the match or swipe direction. **CORE** |
| L or T (5 pieces) | Bomb | Clears the surrounding area. Candy Crush: 3x3, fires twice. Homescapes: larger radius. **CORE** |
| 2x2 square | Homing piece | Flies to one goal or blocker tile and clears it, plus its neighbours on takeoff. **COMMON** (Homescapes paper plane, Candy Crush jelly fish as of 2025) |
| 5 in a line | Colour clearer | Swap with a piece to clear every piece of that colour. **CORE** |

**Combos** (swap two specials together). **COMMON** in Candy Crush and Homescapes. Per the Homescapes wiki, Fishdom only allows the colour+colour combo.
- line + line -> clears one row and one column
- line + bomb -> clears 3 rows and 3 columns
- bomb + bomb -> big blast (5x5 in Candy Crush)
- colour + line or bomb (or homing piece) -> every piece of one colour turns into that special and fires
- colour + colour -> clears the whole board (Candy Crush) or one layer everywhere (Homescapes)
- homing + line or bomb -> carries that special to a goal tile and fires it there

Sources: https://candycrush.fandom.com/wiki/Special_candies ; https://homescapes.fandom.com/wiki/Power-ups (both read via the backup reader)

## 6. Blockers (generic types)
- Layered tile under a piece (needs N matches on it) **COMMON**
- Locked piece / chain / ice: match it once to free it, and it cannot move until then **COMMON** (Fishdom's original "locked tiles")
- Box / crate occupying a cell: nearby matches chip away its layers **COMMON**
- Spreading blocker that grows if left alone **COMMON**, unverified per-game
- Hard-shell blocker that line clearers stop at (Candy Crush liquorice) **COMMON**
- Collectible that must fall to the bottom **COMMON**
- Walls between cells (Homescapes) **COMMON**, unverified detail
- Vases / eggs (Royal Match) **EXTRA (Royal Match)**

## 7. Progression
- Linear level map, played in order; replay allowed (Candy Crush). **CORE**
- Episodes of ~15 levels (Candy Crush). Each new world introduces a new mechanic. **COMMON**
- Scale: Candy Crush passed 2,000 levels by 2016 and 10,000+ since. Royal Match had 12,400+ levels in Nov 2025, adding new ones about every 2 weeks. **COMMON** (endless content)
- Difficulty ramp: tutorial unlocks (rocket at level 2, bomb at 3, plane at 4, rainbow at 5 in Homescapes); levels flagged "hard" or "super hard". **COMMON**
- Gating episodes behind friend requests or payment (old Candy Crush, later a 3-day wait) **EXTRA (Candy Crush)**

## 8. Boosters
**Pre-level** (pick up to 3; Homescapes): start with a bomb + line clearer, start with a colour clearer, double homing pieces. Each costs 900-1,900 coins per 3. **COMMON**
**In-level** (no move spent; Homescapes): hammer (clear one tile), row clear, column clear, shuffle, free swap / glove (swap any two neighbours). 1,900-2,900 coins per 3, unlocking from levels 14-34. **COMMON**
Royal Match in-level: hammer, arrow, cannon, jester's hat. **COMMON**

## 9. Lives
5 lives. A failed level costs 1. One life refills every 30 min (Candy Crush). Refills are bought with currency, asked from friends, or granted as timed "unlimited lives". **COMMON** (Candy Crush, Royal Match, Homescapes, Fishdom events)

## 10. Meta layer
- Renovate or decorate a space with stars from levels: a to-do list of tasks with a choice of designs (Homescapes, Gardenscapes, Royal Match castle). **COMMON**
- Aquariums with fish, pets and decorations, bought with coins **EXTRA (Fishdom)**
- Story chapters, characters, letters that give bonuses (Homescapes) **COMMON**
- Teams / alliances with shared rewards (Royal Match, Homescapes) **COMMON**
- Mini-games ("save the fish" pin puzzles in Fishdom ads and reviews) **EXTRA (Fishdom/Playrix)**, partly unverified
