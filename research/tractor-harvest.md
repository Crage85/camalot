# Harvest-and-Upgrade Vehicle Games: Genre Feature List

Research date: 2026-11-16. Quick pass, built from store pages, portal pages, and one fan guide. CC played none of these games. Mechanics are described in CC's own words.

Tags: **CORE** = the game does not work without it. **COMMON** = most games in the genre have it. **EXTRA** = one game only (game named). **unverified** = CC could not confirm it from a fetched page.

## Core loop
The player drives a vehicle over a field, and crops are cut automatically on contact. A load meter fills. At 100%, cutting stops or extra crops are lost. The player drives back to the base or barn to unload, and the load turns into cash. Cash buys upgrades (faster, carries more, cuts wider). Hitting a cash or progress threshold unlocks a farther or bigger field with a more valuable crop. Repeat.

## Controls
- Keyboard: WASD or arrow keys. Forward/reverse plus turn (tank/car steering) in Harvest Simulator. Free 8-direction move in Harvest Loop. **COMMON**
- Mouse: click-drag to steer (Lumber Harvest). Mouse for shop and upgrade menus. **COMMON**
- Touch: virtual joystick or drag-to-steer. One-touch steering in some mobile games. **COMMON**
- Camera toggle, wide or close (E key, Harvest Simulator). **EXTRA (Harvest Simulator)**

## Rules
- **Harvesting:** crops are cut when the vehicle's cutter passes over them. The skill is route planning: straight rows, inward spirals, going for dense clusters. **CORE**
- **Carry capacity:** a load meter with a hard cap. When full, the cutter stops (Harvest Simulator per browsergamers) or crops are lost (per gardenhorizons guide). A fan guide suggests heading home at about 80-85% full. **CORE**
- **Selling / return to base:** drive into the barn or drop zone to unload, and the load converts to cash automatically. **CORE**
- **Money:** one soft currency (coins/cash). Higher-tier crops pay more per unit. **CORE**
- **Unlocking new tiles/areas:** new fields unlock at earnings milestones (Harvest Simulator), by buying the next area (Lumber Harvest, Farm Crusher), or by reaching a player level (Farm Crusher Area 3 = level 20). **COMMON**
- **What gates a new tile:** usually a cash or earnings threshold. Sometimes a level or stat threshold, and sometimes the vehicle must be able to cut the new crop or tree type (implied by Lumber Harvest saw upgrades; unverified). **COMMON**
- **"Clear the whole field first" requirement:** Craig describes it. CC did not confirm it as a hard rule in any listing. Harvest! (Ander Kumm, iOS) frames each level as clearing a field. **unverified as a gate**
- **Timed sessions:** some variants give a timer per field (Harvester Cut Grass, web). **EXTRA**

## Upgrade tree
| Upgrade | Effect | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | Faster driving, so less time on trips | CORE |
| Capacity / storage / bag / truck | More load per trip | CORE |
| Cut width / blade size | More rows per pass | COMMON (Harvest Simulator, Farm Crusher, Harvest It! "Big Scythe") |
| Cutting power / damage / saw | Cuts tougher crops or trees faster, which unlocks new resource types | COMMON (Lumber Harvest saw, Farm Crusher cutting power, Harvest It! x2 damage) |
| "Efficiency" | Exact meaning unverified (probably yield per crop) | EXTRA (Harvest Loop) |
| New crop/resource types | Higher-value crops unlock as you progress | COMMON |
| New vehicles | Collectible or unlockable harvesters (18 in Harvest!). Unclear whether they change stats | EXTRA (Harvest!, iOS) |
| Vacuum / pickup radius | Pulls in cut crops from farther away | EXTRA (Farm Crusher) |
| Workers / helpers | Idle farmers or pets that harvest for you | EXTRA (Harvest It! "Extra Farmer"). Idle-tycoon cousins (Idle Harvester) are fully automated |
| Farm upgrades | Upgrade the farm itself | EXTRA (Harvest It!). Details unverified |

**Cost scaling:** each level costs more than the last. Common idle-game practice is exponential (about x1.1-1.5 per level). The exact curves are **unverified** for every game listed. Balancing tip from a fan guide: speed without capacity wastes time on trips, so players alternate between the two.

## Progression
- **Map layout:** a sequence of fields or areas of growing size around or away from a base, unlocked one by one. Seasonal fields in Harvest Simulator. **COMMON**
- **Resource types per area:** Harvest Simulator lists wheat, corn, sunflowers, cabbages, tomatoes, and pumpkins (per the fan guide). Lumber Harvest uses trees. Farm Crusher lists wheat, grass, leaves, and roots. Which crop belongs to which area is **unverified**.
- **Idle/offline earnings:** present in mobile titles (Harvest It! offline coins). Not confirmed in the web titles. **COMMON (mobile) / unverified (web)**
- **Session length:** short. A few minutes per field, and play continues without end. Exact figures **unverified**.
- **Endgame:** reviews say progress gets repetitive or hits a wall (Harvest It!, Farm Crusher). **COMMON complaint**

## Power-ups and boosts
- Special plants in the field give a temporary boost (Harvest!, iOS). **EXTRA**
- Permanent multipliers: x2 coins, x2 crops, x2 damage, infinite bag (Harvest It!, sold as IAP). **EXTRA as IAP; multipliers COMMON in idle games**
- Autodrive mode (Harvest!, iOS). **EXTRA**
- Temporary speed or money boosts from ads: **unverified** for these games, but standard in hypercasual games.

## Cosmetics
- Unlockable harvesters/vehicles (Harvest!, 18 skins). **EXTRA**
- Choosing a harvester (Harvester Cut Grass). **EXTRA**
- Pets: idle farmers shown as pets (Harvest It!). **EXTRA**
- Otherwise cosmetics are thin in this genre. **unverified** beyond these.
