Related titles: Donut County (2018) is the premium, story-driven ancestor that Hole.io was accused of cloning. Idle Hole (Voodoo) is an idle/upgrade spinoff.

**Best match for Craig's description:** Hole.io. The tiny hole eats bins, poles and people, then fences and benches, then cars, then whole building blocks. Hole.io has a city map and an added farm scene. Hole Feast also has city and farm maps.

## Core loop
**CORE.** The player steers a hole across a 3D map. Any object smaller than the hole's opening drops in, scores points and grows the hole. Objects that are too big block the hole or roll off. Growth unlocks the next size class of objects (props, then vehicles, then buildings). The round ends on a timer or when only one hole is left. Most games then pay coins toward permanent upgrades.

## Controls
- Touch: drag anywhere to steer (a relative virtual joystick). **CORE**
- Desktop: WASD or arrow keys, or mouse drag/follow. **COMMON** (Hole.io web, Hole Arena, Hole Feast, Voxel hole, Holey.io)
- Speed-boost button (hold left-click or Space, or tap an on-screen button). **EXTRA:** Holey.io (a 2-second burst on a 6-second cooldown)

## Rules
- **Size gate:** an object falls in only if it is smaller than the hole. Big objects block or jam the hole, and swallowing too many large items at once slows you. **CORE**
- **Angle matters:** big items must be approached at the right angle to tip in. **COMMON** (Hole Arena states this; the others imply it through physics)
- **Growth curve:** each swallowed item adds growth, roughly by its size. Hole.io runs about 19+ size levels (a reviewer cites "level 19"). Hole.io's web build grows the hole in steps after you meet an object-count goal (10 objects at level 1). Exact thresholds are **unverified**. **CORE**
- **Speed scaling:** fan guides say the hole gets faster as it grows. **Unverified**
- **Timer:** rounds of about 2 minutes (Hole.io, Hole Arena) or 90 seconds (Hole Feast). Voxel hole uses a per-level time limit. **COMMON**
- **Rival holes:** a bigger hole eats a smaller one. Equal-sized holes cannot eat each other. **COMMON**
- **Respawn:** an eaten hole respawns after a delay (PocketGamer cites a 40-second example) and loses round time. **EXTRA:** Hole.io (other games end your round instead)
- **Win/lose:** biggest hole when time runs out (Classic), last hole alive (Battle), or clear about 100% of the map or meet the objectives before time runs out (Solo/levels). **CORE**
- **Scoring:** points per object, scaled by size. Map-percent meter in Solo. **COMMON**
- **Combos:** chaining swallows builds a combo. **EXTRA:** Hole Feast
- **Shrinking safe zone:** a dead zone closes in on the map. **EXTRA:** Holey.io
- **Banned targets:** swallowing people ends the run. **EXTRA:** Gobble (Poki; not surveyed in depth)

## Game modes
- Classic timed battle (biggest hole after 2 minutes, against bots). **COMMON**
- Battle / battle royale (last hole standing). **COMMON** (Hole.io, Holey.io)
- Solo clear-the-map against the clock. **COMMON** (Hole.io, Hole Feast)
- Objective/level mode (e.g. "eat a landmark", "eat N cars", or hit an object target to level up), with 3-star ratings. **COMMON** (Hole.io web, Hole Arena, Voxel hole)
- Endless/practice (no timer; stars cannot be spent). **EXTRA:** Hole Arena, Voxel hole (unlocks after all 50 levels), Hole.io (a Steam achievement mentions it, **unverified**)
- Local multiplayer on one computer. **EXTRA:** Eat the World (Poki; adjacent genre)

## Progression: maps, difficulty, session length
- Themed maps: city (always the starter), farm, picnic, park, desert, food, outer space, New York, pirate cove, sci-fi city. **COMMON**
- Map unlocks: 15 worlds, City free, others bought with coins (1,200/1,600/2,400). **EXTRA:** Hole Arena
- Handcrafted levels: 50 levels across 5 worlds. **EXTRA:** Voxel hole
- Map randomization (layout differs each match). **EXTRA:** Hole.io (per one review, **unverified**)
- Difficulty: rises through level or objective progression and stronger bots. No difficulty setting was found; a Hole.io reviewer asked for one. **Unverified**
- Session length: 90 seconds to 2 minutes per round. **COMMON**

## Power-ups and boosts (in-match pickups)
- Magnet (pulls nearby items in). **COMMON** (Holey.io, Hole Arena, Voxel hole)
- Speed burst. **COMMON** (Holey.io, Hole Arena)
- Size Up / Get Bigger (instant growth). **COMMON** (Holey.io, Hole Arena, Voxel hole)
- Shrink: Hole Arena lists it as an upgradable power-up; CC assumes it shrinks rivals (**unverified**). Holey.io has a size-down sign to avoid. **COMMON**
- Freeze (stops the clock). **EXTRA:** Voxel hole
- Compass (points to the last remaining items). **EXTRA:** Voxel hole
- Score multiplier pickup (2x star) and hidden high-value gems. **EXTRA:** Idle Hole

## Upgrades, meta progression, currencies
- Soft currency (coins) earned per round from placement, wins or objectives. **COMMON**
- Permanent upgrades:
  - Start size, speed and time. **COMMON** (Hole Feast; ifanzine guide for a similar game)
  - Power-up tiers: Magnet/Speed/Shrink 5 tiers at 50 coins, Get Bigger 3 tiers at 1,000 coins. **EXTRA:** Hole Arena
  - Hole size, gravity pull, gold multiplier, boost tank/regen, offline earnings. **EXTRA:** Idle Hole
- Objective bonus (600 coins for the round objective, far more than winning). **EXTRA:** Hole Arena
- Collectibles: hidden spheres in levels open into figurines for a themed album, and duplicates convert to coins. **EXTRA:** Hole.io (version notes 2.48 to 2.51)
- Premium currency: gems in Hole.io (sold at 320 for $1.99). What gems buy is **unverified**. A "Skip It's" currency was reported as an alternative to watching ads (**unverified**).

## Opponents
- **Bots, not live players.** Hole.io Classic and Battle use AI (Wikipedia; critics called it misleading "multiplayer"). Hole Arena and Hole Feast are AI only, with 3 rival holes in Hole Feast. **COMMON**
- Bot behavior, from fragmentary evidence: bots roam, eat props to grow, and chase smaller holes. Hole.io reviewers call the AI too easy, and say AI and player often stall at equal size where neither can eat the other. Detailed AI logic is **unverified**; Camalot will need to design its own (wander, eat the nearest edible item, chase smaller holes, flee bigger ones).
- Rivals have fake player names. **Unverified**

## Cosmetics
- Hole skins or rim skins (8 in Hole Feast, 80+ in Hole Arena at 500 to 1,000 coins). Skins are cosmetic only. **COMMON**
- Paid skins (Hole.io: Ring of Fire, Rainbow, Swamp Monster at $0.99, plus $4.99 bundle "offers"). **EXTRA:** Hole.io
- Trails. **Unverified** (fan guide only)

## Monetization and retention hooks: what the player gets, and the Camalot swap
| Hook | Seen in | Player gets | Camalot swap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rewarded ad on "Time's up" | Hole.io web (CrazyGames) | +30 seconds to keep playing | Answer a question for +30 seconds (a revive) |
| "Half time" ad mid-round | Hole.io (No Half time IAP $4.99) | Nothing; it is a forced break that also gives extra time per reviews (**unverified**) | Drop it, or make it an optional question break for bonus time |
| Rewarded ad after match for extras | Hole.io (reviews) | Extra rewards (likely doubled coins, **unverified**) | Question streak doubles coins |
| Interstitial ads between rounds | Hole.io (25 to 30 seconds, unskippable, per reviews) | Nothing | Remove |
| Banner ads | Hole.io (removed in later versions) | Nothing | Remove |
| No Ads IAP ($9.99) | Hole.io | No interstitial ads | Not needed |
| Gem packs IAP | Hole.io | Premium currency | Gems earned from hard questions |
| Skin IAP and bundle offers | Hole.io | Cosmetic skins | Unlock skins with question milestones |
| Timed bonus button plus ad multiplier | Idle Hole | Free coins, multiplied by an ad | Question to multiply the bonus |
| Offline earnings | Idle Hole | Coins while away | Skip (not needed offline), or award on return after a quiz |
| Coin-bought upgrades/maps | Hole Arena, Hole Feast | Start size, speed, time, power-up tiers, new worlds | Keep, with coins earned from questions as well as play |
