## Core loop

You steer a worm that never stops moving through a large open arena. Glowing food pellets are scattered around, and eating them makes you longer. Any worm whose head touches another worm's body dies and turns into a trail of big, high-value pellets. Players grow by grazing and by tricking rivals into crashing, often by circling them or cutting in front of them. A run lasts until you die, and your length is your score.

## Rules

| Feature | Detail | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Head-on-body death | Your head touching another worm's body kills you. Your own body is safe in Slither.io. | CORE |
| Death drops food | A dead worm becomes large, bright pellets worth more than normal ones. | CORE |
| Border death | Hitting the arena edge kills you. In Slither.io this leaves no pellets. Worms Zone also counts walls. | COMMON |
| Score = length/mass | No win state; you play for the biggest size you can reach before dying. | CORE |
| Live leaderboard | Top 10 by mass (Slither.io); top 9 plus yourself (Worms Zone). | COMMON |
| Encircle kill | Wrapping your body around a rival traps it until it crashes. This is a tactic, not a separate rule. | COMMON |
| Fleeing high-value pellets | Some pellets run from nearby worms and are worth more when you boost to catch them. EXTRA: Slither.io. | EXTRA |
| Timed mode | A 7-minute mode that pays double points and coins. EXTRA: Worms Zone. | EXTRA |

## Power-ups and boosts

| Feature | Detail | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Boost costs length | Boosting burns your length, and the lost length drops behind you as small pellets. Worms Zone charges more the bigger you are; Slither.io's cost per second is a constant amount (fan source). Snake.io calls it spending energy from food. | CORE |
| Pickup potions | Six arena pickups: wider food pull (magnet), 5x food value, map marker showing where worms died, speed, sharper turning, and zoom-out. Potion colors differ between sources. EXTRA: Worms Zone. | EXTRA |
| Store-listed power-ups | Magnet, speed, and zoom are listed. EXTRA: Worms io Zone (Appcelent, a clone). | EXTRA |
| Wormate.io power-ups | Reported as 7, including magnet and a 2x/5x/10x food multiplier. Unverified. | EXTRA (unverified) |
| Starting boosters by worm rarity | Rarer worms begin each match with more boosters. EXTRA: Worms Zone. | EXTRA |
| Pets with abilities | Pets are collected as random cards and upgraded in a Kennel. Details unverified. EXTRA: Little Big Snake. | EXTRA |
