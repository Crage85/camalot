## 2. Core loop
The crowd runs forward on its own down a narrow track. The player only steers left and right. Pairs of gates show math ("+20", "x2", "-10", "÷2"); passing through one changes the crowd size. Traps along the way kill men. Enemy crowds block the path, and fights trade men one for one. At the end, the surviving crowd fights a boss or storms a castle. Coins earned during the run buy upgrades between runs. **CORE**
